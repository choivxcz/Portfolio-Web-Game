Changes made for the web: only the models used by the site were copied; normal maps were removed and textures were downscaled (256–512 px) to keep downloads small. The character files were trimmed to the body meshes and 8 of their 76 animations (idle, walk, run, jump, interact, cheer) and quantized, bringing each from 3.6 MB to about 430 kB. Model files are otherwise unmodified.

Everything else in the 3D world (terrain, grass, water, sky, broadleaf trees, ivy, landmarks, lake town, bridge, village houses) is original and generated in code.
